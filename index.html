How it works

  1. Turn it on

    Press Alt+H on a tab with a video. Huh? starts listening and keeps only the last 25 seconds.

  2. Miss a line

    Press Alt+H again. The video pauses where you are.

  3. Get it

    Read the line in your language, with a short explanation and any tricky words. Tap Listen to hear it.

English and 22 Indian languages

Huh? understands videos in any of these languages, including mixed ones like Hinglish, and explains them in the one you pick. Read aloud is available in 11 of them.
